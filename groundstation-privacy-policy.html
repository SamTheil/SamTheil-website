Ground Station Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

Ground Station is made by Gray Labs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone. Flight logs, device settings, and your location are used only on your device. Your location is used only to orient the map toward your tracker; it is never sent to Gray Labs. Gray Labs does not run servers for this app and receives none of your data.

Bluetooth. The app uses Bluetooth only to communicate with your Gray Labs devices.

Photos. The app saves pictures and videos to your photo library only when you export one.

Google Maps. Maps are provided by Google's Maps SDK, which collects limited diagnostic and usage information (crash and performance data, product interaction, and identifiers) under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing. Files you export are shared only where you choose to send them.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from them.
