Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 16, 2025

This privacy policy applies to Gray Designs Ground Station, an iOS application developed by Gray Designs. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting any information you may provide while using our app.

Information Collection and Use

Gray Designs Ground Station is designed solely to connect to and control model rocket flight computers. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information. Additionally, the app does not connect to external servers or third-party services, ensuring that no data is shared or intercepted.

Data Storage

No user data is stored within the app. All operations occur locally on your device, and no information is uploaded, saved, or transmitted beyond your device.

Third-Party Services

Since the app does not connect to external servers or third-party services, there is no exchange of data with external entities.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated versions will be made available as required. Please review the policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please use the contact form provided elsewhere in the app.
