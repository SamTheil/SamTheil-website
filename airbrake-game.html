Welcome to the Rocket Flight Simulation Game! Here are the rules:

Your goal is to adjust the airbrakes to achieve the target altitude of 800 feet. Use the 'Practice' button to start a simulation run. Adjust the airbrakes using the up and down arrow keys during the flight. Try to match your final altitude to the target as closely as possible.

When you feel ready, use the 'Qualify' button to make your final attempt and get your name on the leaderboard
